PROJECT / CUDA · GPU COMPUTE

Heat, modeled on the GPU.

I built a CUDA heat simulation to learn how a GPU can model a changing physical world in parallel. That experiment turned my curiosity toward high performance computing and computer architecture.

Inspiration

The simple differential equations behind heat diffusion offered a way to connect a physical process I could observe with the computation underneath it.

I wanted to see how a familiar model changes when thousands of calculations can run in parallel.

Process

I implemented a three dimensional heat model with CUDA and explored how the GPU updates many points in the material at once. The project gave me a hands-on way to study parallel computing, memory, and the hardware behind the simulation.

The animation below shows a center slice of the simulated volume as heat spreads over time.

Results

The simulation turns the numerical updates into a visual result, making the diffusion pattern easier to inspect and compare over time.
