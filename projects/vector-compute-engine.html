PROJECT / IN PROGRESS

Vector Compute Engine

A work in progress. I’m building this page as a place to share the questions, experiments, and decisions behind the project as it takes shape.

Inspiration

I have always seen computers as one of the coolest - and perhaps more importantly, most impactful - advancements in today's world. We can make a rock "think"! And even more than ever, we readily have the ability to do so, even in college!

Since I first started playing games, I can't help but think about how millions of transistors are flipping at that moment: some, part of a mega-highway to perform raytracing - others, its full duty to process mouse and keyboard input. Computers are such a clever combination of so many principles from electrical engineering to physics to math to systemsto algorithms and beyond!

Having taken courses in digital logic, embedded systems, circuit theory, and programming, I am more inspired than ever to keep learning about computing, and therefore to take on the challenge of building a very small GPU, specifically an 8-bit vector compute engine capable of matrix calculations, image processing, and DSP!

Process

I began this project in early July. Although I know the fundamentals of computer architecture, digital logic design, and firmware, there is a lot to tackle in a big project like this.

Because of this, I decided to spit up the project into several key parts, and work through each on its own. Here is a general timeline and a simple Gantt Chart I created to keep me on track!
