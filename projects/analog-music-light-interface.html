PROJECT / IN PROGRESS

Analog Music/Light Interface

A work in progress. I’m building this page as a place to share the questions, experiments, and decisions behind the project as it takes shape.

Inspiration

To me, music is one of life's greatest gifts. It is amazing how music can invoke such strong emotions deep within us. From playing piano to producing my own music, I've always sought to learn how music can really move and inspire people.

I've been getting into electronic music recently and, with my studies in circuit theory and AC circuits, I've wondered if I could produce those zappy, sharp tones that make these songs so powerful. With my fun (and amateurish) experience in producing music, I knew producing melodies and chords that are ear candy is more than just sin and sawtooth waves - it includes complex techniques like filtering, transforms, and delays. This project is an attempt to construct real analog circuits from the theory, all while pursuing one of my life's greatest interests, music.

Part of the project is a smalelr project to combine the analog signal processing with lighting - a laser projector I've been wanting to experiment projection mapping with! As audio plays, I want the projector to update real-time with cool projections!

Process

Right now, I am working on the project as I study through my circuit theory class. As I am starting right now, I plan to finish at the end of the semester.
